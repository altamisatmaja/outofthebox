# outofthebox hunt 001, 2026-10-25

> A complete example of an AFTER mode hunt file, so a team can see what the
> agent hands over. The product is **Acme Tickets**, a fictional concert
> ticket store; every name, path and number here is made up.

- **Target:** "Gift a ticket": the gift option at checkout
  (`/checkout`), the recipient's claim page (`/claim/:token`), the gift
  email, and the entry QR code. API and web.
- **Mode:** AFTER
- **Environment:** local development stack, seeded test accounts
  (`buyer@example.test`, `friend@example.test`), one test event, payment
  provider in test mode. Production not touched.
- **Rounds:** 3; the last round found nothing new: yes.

## Round 1: chatter (the list said out loud before testing)

1. The buyer opens the recipient's claim link while signed in. Can the buyer
   claim their own gift, and does the recipient's link then stop working?
2. The recipient claims from two devices at the same moment: one ticket or two?
3. The entry QR code: is it returned by the API before the gift is claimed?
4. Three instalments of 100.00: do 33.34 + 33.33 + 33.33 sum to the total?
5. The service fee: charged once per order, or again for each gifted ticket?
6. A payment confirmation that arrives after the order expired.
7. The same payment confirmation delivered twice.
8. The gift email: in the recipient's language, or the buyer's?
9. Phone width: does the sticky price bar cover the "Claim" button?
10. Gifts created by the previous version: who owns them now?
11. The buyer cancels the gift while the recipient is on the claim page.
12. Every new message present in all four language files.

## Findings

Ranked by impact (O-13). Pick numbers to fix; unpicked numbers stay untouched.

## Damage already done (O-09)

- Local: 3 gifts repaired (recipient set from the gift email address).
- Production: checked by a read only query, with permission; 0 affected rows.

## Not tested, and why (O-16)

- A real phone: emulated widths only.
- Real card payments: provider test mode only.
- Gifting across currencies: the test event is in one currency.

  code and passes now.
