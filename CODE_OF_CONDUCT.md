# Code of conduct

Be kind, be specific, assume good faith.

- Critique work, not people. "This rule misses the case where the link is
  opened by the owner" is useful; "this is bad" is not.
- No harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in issues, pull requests or
  anywhere the project gathers.
- Keep reports about other people's systems out of public issues. outofthebox
  is for testing your own product.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove content that breaks this, and may block
people who keep breaking it. Report a problem by opening a private security
advisory on the repository or contacting the maintainer listed in
`package.json`.
